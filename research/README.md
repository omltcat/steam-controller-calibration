# Research materials

The publishable research record is kept in this directory, including the technical background, reverse-engineering process, firmware analysis, and source provenance. The `research/` directory is included in the project repository and is licensed under the same terms as the rest of this project.

`sources/` and the downloaded firmware/index artifacts are local reference material. They are ignored by the project because they are copies of other projects or vendor material with their own licensing and redistribution terms. The manifest preserves the upstream repository, revision, URL, byte count, and hash needed to reproduce or inspect those references without bundling them in this project.
