# Research materials

The publishable research record is kept in `FINDINGS.md`,
`FIRMWARE_ANALYSIS_6A628345.md`, `manifest.json`, and `fetch_sources.py`.

`sources/` and the downloaded firmware/index artifacts are local reference
material. They are ignored by the project because they are copies of other
projects or vendor material with their own licensing and redistribution terms.
The manifest preserves the upstream repository, revision, URL, byte count, and
hash needed to reproduce or inspect those references without bundling them in
this project.
